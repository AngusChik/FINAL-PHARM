Ordering Sheet color meanings

Reasoning highlights

  • Yellow — Order for stock or Expiring
  • Orange — Order for basket
  • Blue — High urgency; pickup tomorrow
  • Red across the whole row — Back-Ordered

Yellow, orange, and blue highlight only the Reasoning cell. High urgency replaces yellow or orange. Back-Ordered takes priority across the entire row. BLISTER has no separate highlight.

Status colors

  • Pending
  • Back-Ordered
  • Ordered
  • Partially Received
  • Received
  • Ready for Pickup
  • Patient Contacted
  • Picked Up
  • Cancelled
  • Not for Sale (Consult Pharmacist)
  • Custom text

The row shade follows its status, with the Reasoning highlights above taking priority.

Urgency bars beside the name

  • Red · High — tomorrow pickup
  • Amber · Medium — 4 days
  • Green · Low — 1 week
  • Gray · N/A — no urgency

Quantity remaining

  • OUTRed — none remaining
  • LOWYellow — 1 remaining

A blank quantity has no warning color.

Other indicators

Top filters use red for Pending, green for Ordered, amber for Back-Ordered, and gray for Not for Sale. A filled filter is active. The red High urgency filter shows pending items with high urgency.

A green note button means a note is saved. A purple outline marks a selected row. The multicolor G marks an item added from Google Sheet.
